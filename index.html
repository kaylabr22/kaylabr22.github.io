Kayla Brown

I am a second-year PhD student in the Department of Communication at Stanford University, where I am advised by Fred Turner. My research examines how developments in science, technology, and mass media have shaped legal categories and regulatory frameworks in the United States.

I graduated with a B.A. in Media Studies from UC Berkeley and an MPhil in Sociology from the University of Cambridge, where I studied as a Gates Cambridge Scholar.

My academic CV is available on Stanford Profiles, and my full work experience is visible on LinkedIn.
